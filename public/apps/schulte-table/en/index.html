Why Schulte Flow?

Used by pilots and astronauts for attention training, the Schulte Table is a proven scientific method. We've reimagined it with a stunning, modern interface that makes training a daily pleasure, not a chore.

Key Features:

  • Classic Mode: The standard 5x5 grid to build your foundation.
  • Memory Mode: A challenging twist where numbers disappear—train your short-term memory.
  • Infinite Challenge: Grid sizes from 2x2 up to 7x7.
  • Deep Analytics: Visualize your progress with detailed charts and trend analysis.
  • Immersive Design: Beautiful liquid animations, haptic feedback, and a sleek dark mode.
  • Focus Timer: Built-in timer to track your flow state.

Whether you want to improve your reading speed, boost your focus for work, or simply keep your mind sharp, Schulte Flow is your daily mental gym.
