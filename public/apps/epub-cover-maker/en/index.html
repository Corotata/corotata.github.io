EPUB Cover Maker

Edit Title, Author & Metadata

Works on：iPhone / iPad / Mac

Easily create, customize, or replace covers for your EPUB eBooks on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Update book metadata, adjust reading direction, optimize layout, and export or transfer your books effortlessly.

Core Features

Multi-Way Cover Creation & Aspect Ratio Fitting

  • Photo & File Import: Select any high-resolution image from your Photos library or Files app as your cover.
  • Extract In-Book Illustrations: Scan and extract images embedded inside your EPUB to use as a cover with a single tap.
  • Typographic Templates: Generate clean, minimalist, and literary book covers instantly just by typing the title and author.
  • AI Prompt Assistant: Generate targeted cover illustration prompts based on your book details to copy into popular AI image tools.
  • Popular eReader Ratios: Supports Original, 7:10, 2:3, 3:4, and 5:8 aspect ratios with real-time Fit and Fill previews.
  • Smart Image Compression: Automatically compresses oversized cover pictures to preserve crispness while drastically reducing exported EPUB file size.

Standard EPUB Metadata (OPF) & Series Management

  • Complete Book Details: Edit title, author, publisher, genre tags (Subjects), and book synopsis/description.
  • Series & Volume Organization: Set Series Name and Volume Index to keep your book collections neatly organized.
  • Standards Compliant: Strictly complies with EPUB 2 and EPUB 3 OPF standards for universal compatibility across all reading devices.

Typesetting Direction & Paragraph Indentation

  • Horizontal / Vertical Switching: Convert your eBook to authentic right-to-left vertical text (CSS writing-mode) or switch back to horizontal layout with one click.
  • First-Line Indent: Automatically apply a 2-character first-line indent to unindented paragraphs (skips paragraphs that already contain indentation).
  • XHTML Normalization: Cleans and standardizes chapter markup for consistent rendering across different e-readers.

Lossless Export & Convenient Wireless Transfer

  • Local Storage: Save directly to the iOS Files app or macOS folders.
  • Direct App Sharing: Seamlessly open and share your books with Apple Books, Kindle, Kobo, Readmoo, BOOX, and other reader apps.
  • Local Wi-Fi Web Transfer: Built-in local web server lets you transfer EPUBs to e-ink readers via QR code or local browser URL without cables.
  • Kindle Email Assistant: Quickly attach and send finalized EPUB files directly to your Kindle personal documents.

Privacy First & 100% On-Device Processing

  • Completely Local: EPUB extraction, cover replacement, metadata editing, and repackaging occur entirely on your device.
  • Zero Cloud Upload: Your eBooks are never uploaded to any third-party server, safeguarding your reading library and privacy.

FAQ

Can this app edit the body text of chapters or convert TXT/PDF files into EPUB?

No. This app is dedicated to EPUB cover design, metadata editing, reading direction layout, and repackaging. It does not edit chapter text or convert document formats.

How does local Wi-Fi transfer work?

As long as your Apple device and e-reader (Kindle, Kobo, BOOX, etc.) are connected to the same Wi-Fi network, start the transfer mode and scan the QR code or enter the provided URL in the reader's built-in browser to download your EPUB.

Will adding high-resolution covers blow up the EPUB file size?

No. The built-in smart compression automatically optimizes the image file size while keeping visual details razor-sharp on high-DPI reader screens.
