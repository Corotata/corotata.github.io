EPUB Translator

Bilingual eBook, Parallel Text

Works on：iPhone / iPad / Mac

EPUB Translator is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that translates an entire EPUB ebook into another language and exports a new EPUB: a bilingual edition with the original and the translation paragraph by paragraph, or a translation-only edition.

Download on the App StoreIncludes in-app purchases

WHO IT'S FOR

  • Readers who want a book that has no translation in their language yet
  • Language learners who read novels with parallel text, original and translation side by side
  • Fans of foreign novels, web novels and light novels in English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and more

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Import a DRM-free EPUB from Files
  2. Choose the source language and the target language
  3. Choose the layout: original + translation, translation + original, or translation only
  4. Translate the whole book with Google, Microsoft Edge, or a compatible AI service using your own API key
  5. Export the new EPUB and keep reading in Apple Books, Kindle or other reader apps

KEY FEATURES

  • Whole-book translation, not page by page or copy and paste
  • Bilingual dual-language edition or translation-only edition
  • Reads the book title, author, cover and chapters
  • Keeps the original layout, or switch to horizontal or vertical writing
  • Works on iPhone, iPad and Mac

FAQ

Can it translate PDF files?

No. It supports DRM-free EPUB files, not PDF.

Which translation services can I use?

Google, Microsoft Edge, or an AI translation service with your own API key. Translation requires an internet connection, and translation quality depends on the service you choose.

What do I get after translating?

A new EPUB: a bilingual edition with original and translation side by side paragraph by paragraph, or a translation-only edition. You can keep the original layout or switch to horizontal or vertical text.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and Mac.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Works with DRM-free EPUB files. PDF files are not supported.
  • Translation needs an internet connection. AI services need your own API key.
  • Translation quality depends on the service you choose.
