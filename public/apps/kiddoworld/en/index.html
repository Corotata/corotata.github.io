KiddoWorld

Works on：iPhone / iPad

KiddoWorld combines picture-word learning, audio quizzes, vocabulary practice, and pronunciation training in one app. With clear visuals, native-friendly audio, and language switching, it becomes easier to understand, remember, and revisit new words.

Download on the App Store

Unlike many competitors that focus only on English, KiddoWorld also supports Japanese and Cantonese. The same familiar content can be practiced across multiple languages, making repetition more useful and more engaging.

Highlights:

  • Learn words faster with visual cards
  • Strengthen memory through audio choice games
  • Practice pronunciation while listening and repeating
  • Switch between English, Japanese, Cantonese, and more
  • Explore themes like animals, food, vehicles, colors, and daily objects

Ideal for picture vocabulary, early language input, and repeated multilingual learning.
