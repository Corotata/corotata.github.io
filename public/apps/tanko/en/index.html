Tanko

Organize Photos, Create EPUB

Works on：iPhone / iPad

Your Personal Library, Built with Tanko Ever wished you could turn your collection of comics, illustrations, or inspiring images from the web into a beautifully organized ebook? Tanko makes it happen. Inspired by "tankōbon," the Japanese term for a standalone book, Tanko helps you transform scattered images into elegant collections, offering a professional-grade creation and reading experience.

Download on the App StoreIncludes in-app purchases

Key Features

Flexible Import: Batch import from your photo library or use the built-in browser to collect images.

Powerful Organization: Intuitively drag-and-drop to sort pages. Easily crop and rotate images to perfect your layout.

Immersive Reading: Designed for true reading enthusiasts! Enjoy Manga Mode (right-to-left), landscape dual-page view, and light/dark themes for ultimate comfort.

Professional Export: Generate standard EPUB or universal PDF files with a single tap. Share your creations on any device.

FAQ

Which formats can I export?

Generate a standard EPUB or a universal PDF with a single tap.

What are the limits of the free version?

The free version includes project creation, image import, editing and watermarked exports. Pro unlocks unlimited projects, unlimited exports and watermark-free exports.

Where can I import images from?

Batch import from your photo library, or use the built-in browser to collect images from the web.

Does it support manga reading?

Yes. The reader offers Manga Mode (right-to-left), landscape dual-page view and light or dark themes.

Free vs. Pro

Upgrade to Pro: Unlock everything! Enjoy unlimited projects, unlimited exports, and most importantly, watermark-free exports to give your work a truly professional finish.
