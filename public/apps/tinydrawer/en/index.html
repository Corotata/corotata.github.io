TinyDrawer

Works on：iPhone / iPad

Core Value: Turn Fragmented Info into Private Wisdom

Download on the App Store

We believe that every video, podcast, or article you care about holds value not just in itself, but in your thoughts about it. TinyDrawer provides a quiet, focused tool to help you take that final step from "Browsing" to "Keeping".

  • Efficient Distillation: Quickly identify core ideas in a sea of information, never getting lost in lengthy content.
  • Precision Review: Say goodbye to manual seeking. Let every review take you straight to the moment inspiration struck.
  • Independent Thinking: A completely private space for your thoughts, free from public noise.
  • Organized Library: Unify content from different platforms into your own indexable knowledge base.

TinyDrawer: Your All-in-One Media Study Assistant

Key Features:

Smart Transcription, Efficient Screening

  • Voice to Text: Import video or audio to generate verbatim transcripts instantly. No more blind seeking.
  • Global Overview: Scan the full content at a glance, judge its value quickly, and get straight to the dry goods.
  • One-Tap Positioning: Tap any paragraph in the transcript, and the player jumps to that exact spot. "What you see is what you play."

Thought Capsules, Capture Inspiration

  • Flow Recording: Don't interrupt your flow to take notes. Tap "Log Thought" to automatically anchor the current timestamp.
  • Private Space: Here, there are only your thoughts, no noisy arguments. A pure sanctuary for your mind.
  • Context Recall: Every thought is tightly linked to the source. Tap to recreate the context instantly.

Unified Collection, Build Your Study

  • All in One: Support importing links from mainstream video, podcast, and social platforms. Centralize scattered information.
  • Immersive Reading: Intelligently optimize text layout and seamlessly stitch long images/galleries, restoring the most comfortable reading breath.
  • Inspiration Archive: Index every collection for easy future retrieval.

Ultimate Experience, Born for Reading

  • Variable Speed: 0.5x - 2.0x playback control. Master your own learning rhythm.
  • Beautiful Cards: Automatically extract covers and key info to make your knowledge base a delight to view.
  • iCloud Sync: Seamlessly switch between iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Start deep learning anytime, anywhere.

Target Audience:

  • Lifelong Learners strictly pursuing growth.
  • Content Creators building personal knowledge systems.
  • Podcast Listeners who love deep thinking.
  • You, who desire an independent spiritual corner.

Download TinyDrawer, and give a home to the content you cherish.
