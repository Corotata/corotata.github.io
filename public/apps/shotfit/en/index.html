ShotFit

Works on：iPhone / iPad / Mac

Transform boring screenshots into stunning, viral-ready images in seconds.

Download on the App Store

ShotFit is the ultimate tool for developers, designers, and creators to showcase their work. Forget complex photo editors—just drag, drop, and export polished mockups instantly.

KEY FEATURES

  • True 3D Transforms

Go beyond flat images. Rotate your screenshots in X, Y, and Z axes to create professional, depth-defying mockups. Use built-in scene presets like "Isometric" or "Tilt" for instant style.

  • Smart Size Presets

Never crop again. One-click integrated sizing for all major platforms:

  • Social: Instagram, X (Twitter), YouTube, Pinterest, and Bilibili.
  • App Stores: iPhone, iPad, and Mac App Store screenshot guidelines.
  • Standard: 4K, 1080p, and specific aspect ratios.
  • Premium Device Frames

Wrap your shots in sleek Safari or Chrome browser frames. Customize corner radius, padding, and drop shadows to perfection.

  • Stunning Backgrounds

Choose from curated gradients, solid colors, or upload your own images to make your content pop.

  • Rich Text & Layout

Add titles, subtitles, and annotations directly onto the canvas. Drag to move, pinch to scale—total creative control.

WHY SHOTFIT?

  1. Fast: Smart memory remembers your last used style settings. Drag in a screenshot, and it's instantly styled.
  2. Polished: Pixel-perfect rounded corners and studio-grade shadows give your images a premium feel.
  3. Versatile: From Product Hunt launches to Twitter posts, ShotFit handles all your visual assets.

Stop posting raw screenshots. Make your work look as good as it performs with ShotFit.
