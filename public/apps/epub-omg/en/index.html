EPUB OMG

Style novels, chapters & fonts

Works on：iPhone / iPad

Edit an EPUB and make it your own. EPUB OMG is an ebook editor for refining novels, formatting chapters and redesigning covers. Import an existing EPUB, update its text and table of contents, preview your changes and export a new EPUB.

Download on the App StoreIncludes in-app purchases

GIVE STORIES A DISTINCTIVE LOOK

Style conversations with character names, avatars and chat bubbles. Give letters, quotations and extra notes their own appearance. Add illustrations, information cards and annotations to make different kinds of content easier to follow.

EDIT TEXT, CHAPTERS AND BOOK DETAILS

Change chapter titles and body text, organize the table of contents and update the book title, author and other metadata. Use find and replace, correct typos manually or remove unwanted passages. Save your editing project and return to it later.

REFINE YOUR BOOK'S FORMATTING

Adjust heading and body fonts, line spacing, paragraph spacing and indentation. Choose horizontal or vertical text, and convert between Simplified and Traditional Chinese when needed. Import custom fonts and embed the fonts you use in the exported EPUB.

DESIGN COVERS AND CHAPTER OPENINGS

Use cover templates or replace the cover image. Add chapter-opening artwork, edge effects and illustrations to give your book a consistent visual style.

REVIEW AND REMOVE AD TEXT

Find advertising text embedded in a novel. Search within the current chapter or across the book, inspect matches in context and confirm which text to delete. You decide what is removed.

EXPORT, SHARE AND SEND

Preview your edits, generate a new EPUB and save or share it. You can also send it to a Kindle email address. Email transfer requires a receiving address and an account configured in the system Mail app. Delivery depends on the services involved, and layout support varies between reading apps.

STARTING WITH A TXT FILE?

Our companion app, TXT to EPUB, helps turn TXT novels into EPUB files. EPUB OMG focuses on importing and editing existing EPUBs. Choose the tool that matches your starting file.
