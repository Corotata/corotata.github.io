EPUB Cover Maker lets you replace the cover of an existing EPUB on iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Choose from 33 built-in cover templates or use a picture, update details like the title and author, and export a new file for Apple Books.

## Choose a cover source

| Source | Use it when | Check in the preview |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typographic template | The book has no usable artwork, or you want matching covers for a series | Whether you can read the title at thumbnail size |
| Image from Photos or Files | You already have a cover or illustration | Whether cropping cuts off lettering or a face |
| Illustration from inside the EPUB | You want a cover that matches the book's artwork | Whether the composition still works in a portrait frame |

## Replace the cover and tidy up the library entry

1. **Import the existing EPUB.** Check the title and author first. A template can use these fields as cover text.
2. **Pick a template or image.** Use one template across a series and vary the colors or titles so each volume is recognizable. For a picture cover, try a 2:3 frame, then compare **Fit**, which keeps the entire image visible, with **Fill**, which crops to the frame.
3. **Edit the book details.** Set the title, author, publisher, and description as needed. Give books in the same series a matching series name and use the volume field for their order. Individual readers may display those fields differently.
4. **Save and reopen the export.** Export a new EPUB and import it into Apple Books. Look at the shelf thumbnail, verify the book details, and open a chapter. Keep the source EPUB as a backup.

## FAQ

### Can I replace the cover without changing the book title?
Yes. Keep the existing metadata fields and export after setting the new cover.

### Can I make a cover without artwork?
Yes. Pick a built-in template and use the book's title and author. Preview long titles at shelf size before exporting.

### Which setting prevents the image from being cropped?
Use **Fit** to show the full picture. When the image and frame have different proportions, some space may remain around the picture.

### Does the book get uploaded for editing?
The app unpacks the EPUB, edits the cover and metadata, and repackages the book on your device.

### Can I use it to rewrite chapter text?
For edits inside chapters, use an EPUB editor such as EPUB OMG. EPUB Cover Maker handles covers, book metadata, and reading direction.
