# The Battle of the Century: All for a Piece of Meat?

This is a legend about two legendary gluttons. When Straw Hat Luffy meets Son Goku, a Saiyan raised on Earth, an incredible battle ensues over a piece of bone-in meat found in the wilderness.

From the Gum-Gum Fruit to Gear 5 (Nika), from Kaioken to Ultra Instinct, this battle will shake the entire dimension!
